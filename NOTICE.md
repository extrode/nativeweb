## Who the Licensor is

**Wherever `LICENSE.md` says "Licensor", read "Extrode LLC" for this software.**

`LICENSE.md` is the ISL-NETC text reproduced verbatim, as its own *Notice on Reproducing This
License* permits and as §3.1(b) requires. That text was drafted as the Ikhbat Foundation's own
license, so its **§1.2 defines "Licensor" to mean Ikhbat Foundation (ikhbat.org)** — a definition
that is right for the Foundation's projects and wrong for every third party who applies the text
to their own work. The text cannot be edited to fix it: editing would produce a modified ISL
document, which the reproduction notice forbids, and would break the section numbering the rest
of the family refers to. So it is overridden here instead.

The license family states this reading itself. The **ISL-CLA §1.2 note** reads:

> Some ISL texts define "Licensor" as Ikhbat Foundation specifically, because they were drafted
> as the Foundation's own license. Where a third party applies such a text to their own work,
> they are the Licensor for the purposes of this Agreement... A Licensor should say so where it
> adopts this Agreement.

This file is where NativeWeb says so.

## What binds an operator

ISL-NETC is network copyleft. Two obligations reach further than an ordinary permissive license,
and neither is waived here:

- **§3.6 Remote Network Interaction.** Running a modified version of this software as a hosted or
  SaaS offering obliges the operator to offer those users the Corresponding Source of *that exact
  modified version*, at no charge, under ISL-NETC. Never distributing a copy does not avoid it.
- **§§4 and 5 Ethical Use Restrictions.** These bind every recipient, every derivative work and
  every user of a network-operated modified version, and §3.2 forbids relicensing under terms
  that remove, narrow or waive them downstream.
